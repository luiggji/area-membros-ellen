# Responsive specs and responsive QA

| Viewport | Sidebar | Content columns | Hero | Course cards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320–599 | drawer/bottom nav | 1 | text + compact image | 1 |
| 600–959 | collapsed | 1–2 | short | 2 |
| 960–1199 | 216–232px | content fluid | short | 2–3 |
| 1200+ | 232px | max 1180 | short | 3 |

Rules: avoid overflow; no hidden CTA below unsafe fold; readable type; images with aspect-ratio and object-position; touch targets 44px; forms max-width 560px; sticky purchase action only if it doesn't cover content.

Screen captures at 390x844, 768x1024, 1366x768, 1440x900 and keyboard-only review. Do not treat desktop mock as exact pixel target on mobile.
